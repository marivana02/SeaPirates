Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026 — SeaPirates (“we”, “our”, or “the Game”)

This Privacy Policy explains how SeaPirates collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information when you play our web-based game at seapirates.site and use the SeaPirates mobile application available on Google Play.

By registering and playing SeaPirates, you agree to the data practices described in this policy.

1. Data Controller

SeaPirates is operated by an independent developer team. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at: orivane.app@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Data: When you register, we collect your chosen username, email address, and a password (hashed and salted — we never store plain-text passwords).
  • Device ID: A randomly generated device identifier stored in your browser’s localStorage to help secure your session.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Game Progress: Your in-game statistics (level, gold, pearls, XP, elite points, items, equipment, ship upgrades, combat history, quest progress, PvP rankings, boss damage dealt) are stored on our server to persist your progress across sessions.
  • Session Data: Login timestamps, IP addresses, and last-seen timestamps are logged for security and anti-abuse purposes.
  • Push Notification Tokens: If you enable push notifications, we store a Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token on our server to send you game-related notifications.
  • Client Logs: Error logs from the game client may be sent to our server for debugging and stability improvements. These logs contain technical data (browser, device type, error message) and never include your password.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Google Play: If you download the app from Google Play, Google may share anonymized installation and crash data with us through the Google Play Console. We do not receive your name, email, or Google account ID from Google Play.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and maintain your game account
  • To save and load your game progress
  • To send you push notifications (if enabled) — e.g., daily reminders, boss alerts
  • To detect and prevent cheating, botting, and abuse
  • To improve game stability by analyzing error logs
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Server Storage: Your data is stored on secured servers operated by our hosting provider. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS) for data in transit.
  • Password Security: Passwords are hashed using bcrypt or a comparable algorithm. Plain-text passwords are never accessible to anyone, including administrators.
  • Local Storage: The game stores a session token and a cached copy of your player profile in your browser’s localStorage. This data never leaves your device except when communicating with our API.
  • Retention: We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Inactive accounts may be deleted after 12 months of inactivity.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising or any other commercial purposes.

The only exceptions are:

  • Service Providers: Our hosting provider (server infrastructure) and Firebase (push notifications) process data on our behalf under strict contractual terms. They cannot use your data for their own purposes.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

6. Children’s Privacy

SeaPirates is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us immediately so we can delete it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data (you can also do this in-game via Settings).
  • Withdraw Consent: Disable push notifications at any time in Settings.

To exercise these rights, contact us at orivane.app@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and Tracking

SeaPirates does not use cookies for advertising or tracking purposes. We use only technically necessary localStorage (not cookies) to store your session token and game preferences. This data is not shared with any third party.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be notified via in-game announcement or push notification. Continued use of the Game after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact

For any questions, concerns, or data requests:
Email: orivane.app@gmail.com
